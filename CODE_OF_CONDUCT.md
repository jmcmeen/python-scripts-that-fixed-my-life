# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, we as
contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a
harassment-free experience for everyone, and to maintain a straight face while
applying enterprise governance to scripts that are three lines long.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Documenting a one-line script as though it were critical infrastructure.
- Giving feedback with exactly 50 claps.
- Respecting that the weekend is public knowledge.
- Accepting that Catalog Entry 08003 is broken on purpose.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Pointing out, unprompted, that the file could just be renamed normally.
  (A colleague did this once. She has since left the company. We choose to
  believe these events are unrelated.)
- Suggesting the user "just use Notify Me on their phone" as though this were a
  novel observation.
- Opening the `dev/projects/scripts/` folder without a business case.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported by opening an issue. The
maintainer will respond when the algorithm rewards it.
